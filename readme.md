# PSSAR Research Data Analysis

## What is the hardest part?

The hardest part of this project was handling data from multiple sources and different formats (CSV, JSON, Excel) and making sure they were clean and consistent before merging.  
Dealing with missing values and ensuring correct data types required careful preprocessing to avoid incorrect results in the final analysis.

---

## What did the data tell us about the PSSAR research community?

The analysis of the PSSAR research community showed several insights:

- Some researchers contributed significantly more citations than others, indicating a small group of highly influential researchers.
- Funding was not evenly distributed across fields; certain research areas received significantly higher financial support.
- There are researchers who have been active for many years, showing long-term contribution to the community.
- Data quality issues (such as missing funding values) highlight the importance of proper data collection and maintenance.

Overall, the PSSAR research community shows strong research activity but with clear differences in impact and funding distribution.
